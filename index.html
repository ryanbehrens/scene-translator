About

Scene Translator is a communication aid for first responders. It is not a certified medical interpreter. Use a professional interpreter line when one is available, and confirm critical answers (allergies, medications, pregnancy) another way when you can.

Version

Built-in phrases: Spanish, Chinese (Mandarin), Vietnamese, Tagalog, Arabic, Russian, Japanese. Other languages use machine translation and need internet.

Scene Translator is free for first responders and always will be. If it has helped you or your crew, you can chip in toward hosting and translation costs.
